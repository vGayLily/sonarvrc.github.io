# Legacy and Retired Worlds
Worlds not in active use, either due to being old and not up to our standards (i.e. lacking features) or decision of Event Team to retire a world for various reasons - worlds in this category might still be used for special occasions (i.e. Llama's Apartment for Home Gala events).
